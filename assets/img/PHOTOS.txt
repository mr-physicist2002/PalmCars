HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTOS
======================

The site ships with placeholder images so it runs immediately. To use the
5 photos you attached, just OVERWRITE these files (keep the exact names):

  car-1.jpg   →  Bugatti Chiron Pur Sport   (the orange hypercar on cobblestone)
  car-2.jpg   →  Ferrari F40                (the red car on the mountain road)
  car-3.jpg   →  BMW M4 Competition         (the red/orange coupé at the dealership)
  car-4.jpg   →  Porsche Cayman GT4         (the matte-orange car in the forest)
  car-5.jpg   →  McLaren 600LT              (the orange car under the bridge)

Where each one appears on the site:
  • Hero (full screen background) ............ car-5  (McLaren)
  • Performance section image ................ car-2  (Ferrari F40)
  • Models slider (5 cards) .................. car-1 … car-5
  • Gallery .................................. car-1, car-2, car-3, car-4

Tips:
  • Keep the filenames exactly as above (lowercase, .jpg).
  • Landscape photos look best for the hero and performance image.
  • If you want a different car in the hero, change the <img> src in
    index.html (look for `class="hero__img"`).
